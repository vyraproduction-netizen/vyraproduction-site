What we can conclude

The processed clip is four times wider and four times taller than the source. The fabric and zipper appear sharper in a paused frame. Fine texture may have been synthesized by the model rather than recovered from the original scene, and this test cannot establish fidelity to a physical product.

The service labeled the result “4K format” while the selected setting said “1080p.” Because the exported file is square at 1920 × 1920 pixels, we describe its actual dimensions instead of calling it standard 3840 × 2160 UHD video.

Who should wait for another test?

If you need natural motion, accurate product labels or faithful camera footage, this synthetic sample is insufficient evidence.
